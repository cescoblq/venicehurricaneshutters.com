About Venice Hurricane Shutters

Venice Hurricane Shutters is an independent referral service for homeowners in Venice, FL and the surrounding Sarasota County area (including Venice Island, Golden Beach, and Nokomis) who are looking into hurricane shutters.

We are not a shutter manufacturer or installer. We don’t perform any installation work ourselves — we connect homeowners, free of charge, with licensed local installers who sell and install hurricane shutters in the Venice area.

How it works. You submit a short request through our form describing your project. We match that request with installers who work in your area, and they contact you directly — usually within 24 hours — to schedule a free, no-obligation estimate. You’re never obligated to hire anyone through this service.

How we’re funded. The service is free for homeowners. We’re compensated by the participating installers when we send them a qualified request. That arrangement has no effect on the price you’re quoted for your project.

Your information. We share your details only with the installers who prepare your quote — never with unrelated third parties for other purposes. See our privacy policy for the full details, including how we use consent and communication technology like TCPA-compliant contact consent.

Questions about the service? Contact us.
